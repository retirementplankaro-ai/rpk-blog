## PPF kya hai, ek line mein

PPF (Public Provident Fund) Sarkar ki ek **long-term savings scheme** hai. Isme:

- Lock-in **15 saal** ka hota hai
- Saal mein kam se kam **₹500** aur zyaada se zyaada **₹1.5 lakh** daal sakte hain
- Abhi interest rate **7.1% saalana** hai (October–December 2026 quarter ke liye)
- Interest **har saal compound** hota hai, yaani interest par bhi interest milta hai
- Maturity amount aur interest dono **tax-free** hain

Dhyan rahe: PPF ka rate har teen mahine mein Sarkar review karti hai. Ye fixed ya guaranteed nahi hai. Neeche ke saare numbers is assumption par hain ki rate **7.1% hi bana rahe**. Rate badla to final amount bhi badlega.

## 5 tareekh ka rule: chhoti galti, bada nuksaan

PPF mein interest har mahine ki **5 tareekh se mahine ke aakhir tak ke minimum balance** par lagta hai. Iska matlab:

- Agar aap 5 tareekh **tak** paisa daalte hain, to us mahine ka interest milta hai
- Agar 6 tareekh ya baad mein daalte hain, to us mahine ka interest **nahi milta**

Ab dekhiye iska asar, ₹12,500 har mahine (yaani ₹1.5 lakh saal) daalne par, 15 saal baad:

- **1–5 April ko poore saal ka ₹1.5 lakh ek saath:** ₹40,68,209
- **Har mahine 5 tareekh tak ₹12,500:** ₹39,44,599
- **Har mahine 5 tareekh ke baad ₹12,500:** ₹39,22,125

Yaani paisa wahi ₹22.5 lakh, lekin sirf **timing badalne se ₹1.46 lakh ka fark**. Agar ek saath daalna possible nahi hai, to kam se kam har mahine **5 tareekh se pehle** daalne ki aadat daal lein. Salary aate hi auto-debit set kar dena sabse aasaan tareeka hai.

## 15 saal ke baad kya? Extension ka option

Bahut log maturity par poora paisa nikaal lete hain. Lekin PPF ko **5-5 saal ke block** mein aage badhaya ja sakta hai. Do tareeke hain:

1. **Paisa daalte rahein (with contribution):** Iske liye maturity ke ek saal ke andar bank ya post office mein form jama karna hota hai. Upar wali table ka 25 saal wala column isi tareeke se hai.
2. **Bina naya paisa daale (without contribution):** Purana balance pada rehta hai aur us par interest milta rehta hai.

Example: ₹1.5 lakh saal se 15 saal mein ₹40.68 lakh bana. Agar uske baad ek bhi rupaya naya na daalein aur 10 saal chhod dein, to 7.1% par ye lagbhag **₹80.78 lakh** ho jaata hai. Naya paisa daalte rehte to ₹1.03 crore. Dono hi options maturity par sab nikaal lene se kaafi behtar hain, agar us paise ki turant zaroorat na ho.

## Tax ka fayda kisko milta hai?

PPF ko **EEE** kaha jaata hai: jama karne par, interest par aur maturity par, teeno jagah tax nahi.

Lekin ek baat clear kar lein. Jama karne par **Section 80C ki deduction sirf old tax regime** mein milti hai. Agar aap new regime mein hain, to deposit par deduction nahi milegi. Haan, interest aur maturity amount **dono regime mein tax-free** rehte hain.

## PPF kis kaam ke liye sahi hai?

PPF ek **safe, stable hissa** hai, poora plan nahi. Is par 7.1% milta hai, jabki lambi avadhi mein mehngai bhi 5–6% ke aas-paas reh sakti hai. Isliye sirf PPF ke bharose bada goal poora karna mushkil ho sakta hai.

PPF in cheezon ke liye accha fit hai:

- **Retirement corpus ka safe hissa**, jahan market ka utaar-chadhaav nahi chahiye
- **Bachchon ki padhai** jaisa goal jo 15+ saal door ho
- Un logon ke liye jo old regime mein **80C** bharna chahte hain

## FAQ

### PPF mein ₹1.5 lakh saal daalne par 15 saal mein kitna milega?

7.1% rate maan kar, aur har saal 5 April se pehle poora paisa daalne par, 15 saal baad lagbhag ₹40.68 lakh banega. Isme aapka jama ₹22.5 lakh aur interest ₹18.18 lakh hoga. Rate badla to amount bhi badlega.

### PPF ka interest rate abhi kitna hai?

October–December 2026 quarter ke liye PPF ka rate 7.1% saalana hai. Sarkar har quarter rate review karti hai, isliye ye aage badal bhi sakta hai.

### Kya PPF se 15 saal se pehle paisa nikaal sakte hain?

Haan, kuch shart ke saath. Account ke 5 poore financial year ke baad saal mein ek baar partial withdrawal ki suvidha hai, aur shuruaati saalon mein PPF balance par loan bhi mil sakta hai. Exact limit apne bank ya post office se confirm karein.

### New tax regime mein PPF ka koi fayda hai?

New regime mein jama par 80C deduction nahi milti. Lekin PPF ka interest aur maturity amount phir bhi poori tarah tax-free rehta hai, jo ek safe hisse ke liye accha fayda hai.

### Ek saath daalna behtar hai ya har mahine?

Interest ke hisaab se 1–5 April ko ek saath daalna sabse behtar hai. Agar monthly daalte hain to har mahine 5 tareekh se pehle daalein. ₹12,500 mahine par 5 tareekh ke baad daalne se 15 saal mein lagbhag ₹22,000 kam milta hai.
