## NPS kya hai, simple shabdon mein

NPS ek sarkari pension scheme hai jise **PFRDA** regulate karta hai. Aap har mahine ya saal mein paisa daalte hain. Ye paisa equity, corporate bonds aur government securities mein lagta hai.

- 18 se 70 saal tak ka koi bhi Indian khol sakta hai
- Tier 1 account mein paisa 60 saal tak lock rehta hai (kuch partial withdrawal ki chhoot hai)
- Equity ka hissa aap khud chun sakte hain, ya "auto choice" le sakte hain jisme umar ke saath equity kam hoti jaati hai
- Return **market se judaa hai, guaranteed nahi hai**

## 60 par kya milta hai: naye exit rules

December 2025 mein PFRDA ne non-government subscribers (yaani private job aur self-employed) ke liye exit rules badle:

- 60 par corpus ka **80% tak ek saath (lump sum)** nikaal sakte hain
- Kam se kam **20% se annuity** kharidni hogi. Annuity hi aapki monthly pension hai
- Aap chahein to annuity mein 20% se zyaada bhi daal sakte hain, jitna zyaada daalenge, utni zyaada pension

Tax ka dhyan rakhein: 60 par lump sum ka **60% hissa tax-free** hai. Usse upar nikaale gaye hisse ka tax treatment apne CA se confirm kar lein. Annuity se aane wali pension aapke slab ke hisaab se taxable hoti hai.

## Tax mein bhi fayda

NPS ka ek bada plus point tax saving hai:

- **Old regime:** 80C ke ₹1.5 lakh ke upar **80CCD(1B) mein ₹50,000 extra** deduction milta hai. 30% slab wale ka isse lagbhag ₹15,600 tax bachta hai (cess ke saath)
- **New regime:** apna contribution deduct nahi hota, lekin **employer ka NPS contribution (80CCD(2))** basic + DA ke 14% tak tax-free rehta hai. HR se "corporate NPS" ke baare mein poochiye

## FAQ

### NPS mein kam se kam kitna paisa daalna padta hai?

Tier 1 account mein saal mein kam se kam ₹1,000 daalna zaroori hai. Lekin achhi pension ke liye ₹3,000 se ₹5,000 mahina ya usse zyaada daalna behtar rehta hai.

### Kya NPS par return guaranteed hai?

Nahi. NPS ka paisa market mein lagta hai, isliye return upar-neeche hota hai. Is article ke 9% aur 6% sirf assumption hain, guarantee nahi.

### 60 se pehle NPS ka paisa nikaal sakte hain?

Tier 1 se kuch khaas zarooraton jaise bachchon ki padhai, shaadi, ghar ya bimari ke liye partial withdrawal ki chhoot hai, kuch shartein ke saath. Poora paisa aam taur par 60 par hi milta hai.

### Annuity mein 20% daalun ya zyaada?

Ye aapki zaroorat par depend karta hai. Zyaada annuity matlab har mahine pakki zyaada pension, lekin ek saath kam paisa. Agar aapke paas doosri income ka zariya kam hai, to zyaada annuity sochne layak hai.

### Kya NPS akele retirement ke liye kaafi hai?

Zyaadatar logon ke liye nahi. Mehngai ki wajah se pension ki asli value kam ho jaati hai. NPS ko EPF, PPF aur SIP ke saath ek hissa ki tarah use karna samajhdaari hai.
