Yaad kijiye, 15-20 saal pehle ek litre doodh kitne ka tha? Aur aaj kitne ka hai? Yahi hai **inflation (mehngai)**. Ye chupchaap har saal aapke paise ki taakat kam karti rehti hai.

Zyaadatar log retirement plan banate waqt aaj ke kharche ko dekh kar sochte hain, "Mujhe to ₹50,000 mahina kaafi hai." Lekin retirement 20-25 saal baad aayega, aur tab ₹50,000 se aaj jaisa ghar nahi chalega. Is article mein real numbers ke saath dekhte hain ki mehngai aapke retirement ko kitna badal deti hai.

## Inflation kya karti hai, simple bhasha mein

Agar mehngai 6% saal hai, to aaj jo cheez ₹100 ki hai, wo agle saal ₹106 ki ho jaayegi. Sunne mein chhota lagta hai. Lekin ye har saal **compound** hota hai, yaani badhi hui keemat par phir se badhta hai.

Ek example: aaj doodh ₹60 litre maan lijiye. 6% mehngai par **25 saal baad** wahi doodh lagbhag **₹258 litre** hoga.

Isi tarah, aaj ka ₹1 crore 25 saal baad sirf **₹23.3 lakh** jitni cheezein khareed paayega (aaj ki keemat mein). Yaani "1 crore kaafi hai" wali soch purani ho chuki hai.

## Sirf FD mein paisa rakhna kyun kaafi nahi

Maan lijiye FD par 7% interest mil raha hai aur aap 30% tax slab mein hain. Tax ke baad haath mein lagbhag **4.9%** aata hai. Agar mehngai 6% hai, to asal mein aapka paisa har saal **thoda ghat** raha hai.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki FD bekaar hai. Emergency fund aur short-term goals ke liye FD achhi hai. Lekin 20-25 saal ke goal ke liye sirf FD par depend rehna mehngai se haarna hai. Lambe goal ke liye alag-alag asset classes ka sahi mix (equity, debt, EPF/PPF) zaroori hai, apne risk ke hisaab se.

## Mehngai se bachne ke 5 simple tareeke

1. **Plan aaj ke kharche se nahi, future ke kharche se banaayein.** Hamesha 6-7% mehngai jod kar sochein.
2. **Har saal SIP badhaayein.** Salary badhe to SIP bhi 10% badhaayein.
3. **Health insurance jaldi lein.** Medical mehngai aam mehngai se zyaada tez badhti hai.
4. **Saal mein ek baar plan check karein.** Kharche badle to target bhi badlein.
5. **Retirement ke baad bhi kuch hissa growth mein rakhein.** Retirement 25-30 saal lamba ho sakta hai, aur mehngai tab bhi chalti rahegi.

## FAQ

### India mein retirement planning ke liye kitni inflation maan kar chalein?

Lambe samay ke liye 6% ek theek-thaak assumption hai. Safe side rehna ho to 7% bhi maan sakte hain. Health aur education kharche ke liye 8-10% maanna better hai.

### Kya ₹1 crore retirement ke liye kaafi hai?

Zyaadatar families ke liye nahi. 6% mehngai par aaj ka ₹1 crore 25 saal baad sirf ₹23 lakh jitni cheezein khareed paayega. Apna number kharche aur umar ke hisaab se nikaalein.

### Retirement ke baad bhi inflation ka asar hota hai kya?

Haan. Retire hone ke baad bhi kharche har saal badhte hain. Isliye corpus aisa hona chahiye jo 25-30 saal tak badhte kharche jhel sake.

### Inflation se bachne ke liye kahan invest karein?

Koi ek product sahi jawab nahi hai. Lambe goal ke liye equity, debt aur EPF/PPF jaise options ka mix apne risk profile ke hisaab se rakhein. Zaroorat ho to SEBI-registered adviser se baat karein.

### Kya step-up SIP sach mein madad karta hai?

Haan. Amit ke example mein fixed SIP ₹43,090 mahina chahiye tha, jabki step-up SIP sirf ₹17,545 se shuru ho sakta hai. Shuruaat aasaan hoti hai aur salary ke saath badhta hai.
