Emergency Fund kitna hona chahiye? 3, 6 ya 9 mahine ka simple hisaab

26 November 2026 · Retirement Plan Karo

Job chali gayi, ghar mein koi bimaar ho gaya, ya gaadi ka bada repair aa gaya. Aise time pe sabse pehla sawaal hota hai: paisa kahan se aayega? Jinke paas emergency fund hota hai, woh shaanti se situation sambhaal lete hain. Jinke paas nahi hota, woh credit card, personal loan ya apni SIP todne par majboor ho jaate hain.

Is article mein simple hisaab se samjhenge ki emergency fund kitna hona chahiye, kahan rakhna chahiye aur ise step-by-step kaise banayein.

Emergency fund kya hota hai?

Emergency fund woh paisa hai jo sirf achaanak aane wali zaroorat ke liye alag rakha jaata hai. Ye investment nahi hai, ye ek suraksha kavach hai. Iska kaam return kamaana nahi, balki zaroorat ke time turant haath mein aana hai.

Emergency mein ye cheezein aati hain:

  • Naukri chhootna ya salary ruk jaana
  • Medical kharcha jo insurance cover na kare
  • Ghar ya gaadi ka achaanak bada repair
  • Parivaar mein koi zaroori travel

Shaadi, vacation, naya phone ya sale ki shopping emergency nahi hain. Inke liye alag goal banayein.

Short answer: kitna rakhein?

Basic rule: apne zaroori monthly kharche ka 6 guna.

  • 3 mahine: agar ghar mein do log kamaate hain aur job pakki hai (jaise government job)
  • 6 mahine: zyaadatar salaried families ke liye sahi
  • 9–12 mahine: agar ghar mein akele kamaane wale hain, private job hai, ya income har mahine badalti hai (business, commission, freelancing)

Dhyan dein: ye hisaab salary par nahi, kharche par hota hai. Aur kharche mein EMI aur insurance premium bhi gine jaate hain, kyunki job jaane par bhi ye rukte nahi.

Example: Sharma family ka hisaab

Sharma ji Pune mein private job karte hain. Ghar mein wife aur do bachche hain. Unka monthly zaroori kharcha kuch aisa hai:

  • Ghar ka kiraya: ₹15,000
  • Ration aur sabzi: ₹10,000
  • Bijli, phone, internet: ₹4,000
  • Car EMI: ₹12,000
  • Bachchon ki school fees: ₹5,000
  • Insurance premium (mahine ka hissa): ₹2,000
  • Petrol / transport: ₹3,000

Kul zaroori kharcha: ₹51,000 per month.

Sharma ji ghar mein akele kamaate hain aur private job hai, isliye unhe kam se kam 6 mahine ka fund chahiye:

₹51,000 × 6 = ₹3,06,000

Agar woh 9 mahine ka cushion rakhna chahein to ye ₹4,59,000 banega.

Kaise banayein? RD se step-by-step plan

₹3 lakh ek saath nikaalna mushkil hai. Isliye har mahine thoda-thoda jodein. Maan lijiye Sharma ji Recurring Deposit (RD) karte hain aur use 6.5% saalana interest milta hai (ye sirf example ke liye assumption hai, bank ke hisaab se rate alag ho sakta hai). ₹3,06,000 tak pahunchne ke liye:

  • 12 mahine mein: lagbhag ₹24,600/month
  • 18 mahine mein: lagbhag ₹16,100/month
  • 24 mahine mein: lagbhag ₹11,900/month
  • 36 mahine mein: lagbhag ₹7,700/month

Sharma ji ke liye 24 mahine ka plan practical hai: har mahine ₹11,900 aur 2 saal mein fund taiyaar. Apne numbers daal kar dekhne ke liye free RD Calculator try karein.

Tip: Bonus, Diwali gift ya tax refund mile to uska ek hissa seedha emergency fund mein daal dein. Isse fund jaldi banta hai.

Emergency fund kahan rakhein?

Teen cheezein dekhni hain: paisa surakshit ho, jaldi nikle, aur thoda bahut interest mile.

  1. Savings account (alag wala): 1 mahine ka kharcha yahan rakhein, taaki usi din nikal sake. Salary wale account se alag rakhein, warna kharch ho jaayega.
  2. Fixed Deposit (FD): baaki ka bada hissa. Chhoti-chhoti kai FD banayein (jaise 3–4 alag FD), taaki zaroorat ho to sirf ek todni pade.
  3. Liquid ya overnight debt fund: kuch log yahan bhi rakhte hain. Paisa aam taur par 1 working day mein aa jaata hai, lekin return fixed nahi hota.

Kahan na rakhein: shares, equity mutual funds, gold jewellery ya property. Emergency ke time market gira hua ho sakta hai, aur aapko nuksaan mein bechna padega.

Inflation ke saath fund bhi badhaiye

Kharche har saal badhte hain. 6% mehngai maanein to Sharma ji ka ₹51,000 ka monthly kharcha:

  • 3 saal baad lagbhag ₹60,700 ho jaayega, aur 6 mahine ka fund ₹3,64,000 chahiye hoga
  • 5 saal baad lagbhag ₹68,200 ho jaayega, aur fund ₹4,09,000 chahiye hoga

Isliye saal mein ek baar (jaise har April) apna kharcha dobara check karein aur fund ko update karein.

5 galtiyan jo log karte hain

  1. Credit card limit ko emergency fund samajhna: card ka byaaj bahut zyada hota hai. Ye fund nahi, karza hai.
  2. Emergency fund ko invest karke return dhoondhna: iska kaam return nahi, suraksha hai.
  3. Health insurance na lena: ek hospital bill poora fund khatam kar sakta hai. Pehle health insurance, phir fund.
  4. Fund use karke wapas na bharna: emergency ke baad sabse pehla goal fund ko dobara bharna hona chahiye.
  5. Sab paisa ek hi FD mein: chhote kaam ke liye bhi poori FD todni padti hai aur interest kat jaata hai.

FAQ

Emergency fund kitne mahine ka hona chahiye?

Zyaadatar salaried families ke liye 6 mahine ke zaroori kharche ke barabar. Akele kamaane wale, private job ya badalti income wale log 9–12 mahine ka rakhein.

Kya pehle emergency fund banayein ya SIP shuru karein?

Behtar hai ki kam se kam 3 mahine ka fund pehle ban jaaye. Uske baad SIP aur fund dono saath mein chala sakte hain.

Kya emergency fund FD mein rakhna sahi hai?

Haan, FD ek aam aur surakshit option hai. Ek bada FD banane ki jagah 3–4 chhoti FD banayein, aur 1 mahine ka kharcha savings account mein rakhein.

Kya health insurance hone par emergency fund ki zaroorat nahi?

Zaroorat hai. Insurance sirf hospital ka kharcha deta hai. Job jaane par ghar ka kiraya, EMI aur school fees emergency fund se hi chalenge.

Aakhri baat

Emergency fund boring lagta hai, lekin yahi aapki baaki saari planning ko bachata hai. Jab ye fund taiyaar hota hai, to market girne par SIP todni nahi padti aur loan lene ki naubat nahi aati. Aaj hi apna monthly kharcha nikaaliye, use 6 se guna kijiye, aur pehla RD shuru kar dijiye.
