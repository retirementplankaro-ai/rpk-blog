VPF mein ₹5,000 extra daalein to retirement par kitna fayda? Pura hisaab

27 November 2026 · Retirement Plan Karo

Har mahine salary slip mein ek line hoti hai: "PF deduction". Zyaadatar log ise bas ek kati hui amount samajh kar aage badh jaate hain. Lekin isi PF account mein ek chhupa hua option hai jiska naam hai VPF (Voluntary Provident Fund). Thoda sa extra paisa isme daalne se retirement corpus mein lakhon ka fark aa sakta hai.

Is article mein real numbers ke saath samjhenge ki VPF kya hai, kitna fayda hai, aur kin logon ke liye ye sahi baith sakta hai.

VPF kya hai, ek line mein

Aapki basic salary (plus DA) ka 12% EPF mein compulsory katta hai. Agar aap apni marzi se isse zyaada katwana chahein, to wo extra hissa VPF kehlaata hai.

  • VPF aapke usi EPF account mein jaata hai, alag account nahi khulta
  • Interest rate EPF jitna hi milta hai. FY 2025-26 ke liye ye 8.25% declare hua hai
  • Aap basic + DA ka 100% tak VPF mein daal sakte hain
  • Company VPF par koi matching contribution nahi deti. Extra paisa sirf aapka hota hai

Dhyan rahe: EPF ka rate har saal EPFO aur Sarkar decide karti hai. Ye guaranteed nahi hai. Neeche ke saare numbers is assumption par hain ki rate 8.25% hi bana rahe.

Rahul ki kahani: ₹5,000 ka asli asar

Rahul 30 saal ka hai. Uski basic salary ₹40,000 hai aur har saal lagbhag 5% badhti hai.

  • Uska apna EPF: 12% = ₹4,800 mahina
  • Company ka EPF hissa: ₹3,550 mahina (baaki ₹1,250 pension scheme EPS mein jaata hai)

Ab do raaste dekhiye, 60 saal ki umar tak (8.25% rate maan kar):

  • Sirf normal EPF: retirement par lagbhag ₹2.21 crore
  • Normal EPF + ₹5,000 VPF har mahine: lagbhag ₹2.95 crore

Yaani ₹5,000 mahine ki aadat ne corpus mein ₹74 lakh jod diye. Ye paisa sabse safe category ke instruments mein se ek mein badha, bina market ke utaar-chadhaav ke.

Rahul ko kitna total corpus chahiye, ye jaanne ke liye wo Retirement Planner mein apne kharche aur umar daal sakta hai.

Tax ka hisaab: ek limit yaad rakhein

VPF ke saath tax ki teen baatein samajh lijiye:

  1. Old tax regime: EPF + VPF dono Section 80C mein aate hain (total ₹1.5 lakh tak). New regime mein ye deduction nahi milta.
  2. Interest tax-free, ek limit tak: Agar aapka apna EPF + VPF milakar saal mein ₹2.5 lakh se zyaada ho jaata hai, to us extra hisse ke interest par tax lagta hai.
  3. Maturity: 5 saal ki continuous service ke baad nikaala gaya paisa tax-free hota hai.

Rahul ke case mein uska apna EPF + VPF saalana ₹2.5 lakh ki limit ko lagbhag 26ve saal mein cross karta hai. Uske baad sirf extra hisse ke interest par thoda tax lagega. Upar ke numbers mein ye tax nahi ghataya gaya hai, isliye asli amount thoda kam ho sakta hai.

VPF kaise shuru karein

Process kaafi simple hai:

  • Apne HR ya payroll team ko VPF ke liye likh kar ya email karke bataiye
  • Kai companies mein ye option salary portal par hi hota hai
  • Amount ya percentage batana hota hai, jaise "basic ka 5% extra"
  • Aksar ye change financial year ki shuruaat (April) mein hota hai, par kuch companies beech mein bhi allow karti hain

Ek baar shuru karne ke baad kai companies poore saal ke liye amount fix rakhti hain. Isliye utna hi chuniye jitna aaram se kat sake.

VPF kiske liye sahi, kiske liye nahi

Sahi baith sakta hai agar: - Aap safe, fixed-type return chahte hain aur market ka risk kam lena hai - Aapka emergency fund aur term insurance pehle se set hai - Aap paisa retirement tak bhoolna chahte hain

Soch kar lein agar: - Aapko agle 5-7 saal mein ghar, padhai jaise bade kharchon ke liye paisa chahiye. VPF se paisa nikaalne ke rules hain, ye bank account jaisa flexible nahi - Aapki umar kam hai aur aap lambe time ke liye kuch hissa equity mein bhi rakhna chahte hain. FAQ

VPF par interest rate kitna milta hai?

VPF par wahi rate milta hai jo EPF par. FY 2025-26 ke liye ye 8.25% hai. Rate har saal declare hota hai, isliye aage badal bhi sakta hai.

Kya company VPF par bhi apna hissa deti hai?

Nahi. Company sirf compulsory 12% par apna hissa deti hai. VPF ka extra paisa poori tarah aapka apna hota hai.

Kya VPF beech mein band ya kam kiya ja sakta hai?

Haan, HR ko bata kar band ya kam kiya ja sakta hai. Lekin kai companies saal mein ek hi baar change allow karti hain, isliye apni company ki policy check kar lein.

VPF ka paisa kab nikaal sakte hain?

VPF ka paisa EPF ke rules se hi nikalta hai. Retirement, naukri chhootne, ya ghar, shaadi, ilaaj jaise kuch khaas kaamon ke liye partial withdrawal allowed hai.

New tax regime mein VPF ka koi fayda hai?

New regime mein 80C deduction nahi milta. Lekin interest ₹2.5 lakh ki contribution limit tak tax-free rehta hai, isliye safe savings ke roop mein VPF tab bhi kaam ka ho sakta hai.
