1 Crore ke liye kitna SIP karna padega? 10, 15, 20, 25 saal ki table

25 November 2026 · Retirement Plan Karo

"1 crore" bahut logon ka pehla bada financial goal hota hai: bachchon ki padhai, ghar, ya retirement ka base. Achhi baat ye hai ki sahi time se shuru karein to ye goal ek normal salary se bhi possible hai. Is article mein simple table se samjhenge ki 1 crore ke liye har mahine kitna SIP karna hoga.

Short answer

Agar aap 12% saalana return maan kar chalein (equity mutual funds ka lamba-time ka average assumption), to 1 crore ke liye:

  • 10 saal mein: lagbhag ₹43,000/month
  • 15 saal mein: lagbhag ₹19,800/month
  • 20 saal mein: lagbhag ₹10,000/month
  • 25 saal mein: lagbhag ₹5,300/month

Yaani jitna jaldi shuru, utni chhoti SIP.

Poori table: 10% vs 12% return

Har mahine ki shuruaat mein SIP maan kar calculate kiya gaya hai. Amounts round off kiye gaye hain.

Dhyan dein: 10 saal se 20 saal karne par SIP lagbhag 4 guna kam ho jaati hai. Yahi compounding ki taakat hai: waqt aapka sabse bada dost hai.

Example: Rahul aur Amit

Rahul ne 25 saal ki umar mein ₹5,300 ki SIP shuru ki aur 25 saal chalayi. Kul jama kiya: lagbhag ₹15.9 lakh.

Amit ne 40 saal ki umar mein shuru kiya aur 10 saal mein 1 crore chahiye tha, to use ₹43,000 ki SIP karni padi. Kul jama kiya: lagbhag ₹51.6 lakh.

Dono ke paas end mein lagbhag 1 crore hai, lekin Amit ko apni jeb se 3 guna se zyada paisa daalna pada. Fark sirf shuru karne ke time ka hai.

Step-up SIP: chhoti shuruaat, bada result

Agar abhi badi SIP possible nahi hai, to step-up SIP sabse practical tareeka hai: har saal salary badhne ke saath SIP ko 10% badha dein. Isse shuruaati SIP kaafi kam rakh kar bhi goal tak pahuncha ja sakta hai, aur jeb pe zyada bojh nahi padta. Apne numbers ke saath try karne ke liye free SIP Calculator use karein.

Inflation ko mat bhooliye

Aaj ka 1 crore, 20 saal baad aaj jitna nahi lagega. 6% mehngai maanein to 20 saal baad ke 1 crore ki value aaj ke hisaab se lagbhag ₹31 lakh jitni hogi. Isliye retirement plan banate waqt sirf "1 crore" nahi, balki aapke kharchon ke hisaab se zaroori amount nikaalna chahiye. Iske liye free Retirement Planner 2 minute mein aapka number bata deta hai.

4 galtiyan jo log karte hain

  1. Market girne par SIP band karna: girte market mein hi sasti units milti hain. Band karne se nuksaan hota hai.
  2. Bahut zyada return maan lena: 15–18% maan kar plan banaoge to goal miss ho sakta hai. 10–12% safe assumption hai.
  3. Step-up na karna: salary badhti hai, SIP wahi rehti hai.
  4. Goal ke paas aakar bhi poora paisa equity mein rakhna: goal se 2–3 saal pehle dheere-dheere safe options mein shift karein.

FAQ

1 crore ke liye 20 saal mein kitna SIP chahiye?

12% saalana return maan kar lagbhag ₹10,000 per month. 10% return par ye lagbhag ₹13,100 per month hoga.

Kya ₹5,000 ki SIP se 1 crore ban sakta hai?

Haan, agar aap ise lagbhag 25–26 saal tak 12% average return par continuously chalate hain. Step-up SIP se ye time aur kam ho sakta hai.

12% return kya guaranteed hai?

Nahi. Mutual fund returns market par depend karte hain aur upar-neeche hote rehte hain. 12% sirf ek lamba-time ka assumption hai, guarantee nahi.

SIP kis fund mein karein?

Ye aapki umar, goal aur risk lene ki kshamata par depend karta hai. Kisi SEBI-registered adviser se baat karein, aur apni situation ke liye free calculator se pehle apna target number zaroor nikaalein.
